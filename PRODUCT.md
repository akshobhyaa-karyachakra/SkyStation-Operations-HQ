# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal operations teams and customer-facing teams use the portal to review delivery, assets, crew, analytics, billing evidence, and customer-safe MIS packs. They need to understand current status, inspect supporting evidence, and prepare clear follow-up actions without exposing unsupported or internal-only data.

## Product Purpose

SkyStation Operations HQ is a shared operational review surface for Skylark teams. It brings activity execution, inventory readiness, crew context, analytics, billing evidence, and customer MIS reporting into one navigable workspace with traceable records and clear source boundaries.

## Brand Personality

Precise, field-tested, practical. The interface should support confident decisions in operational reviews and remain clear enough for customer-facing presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard styling, excessive blue gradients, decorative charts without traceability, dense spreadsheet-like screens with weak hierarchy, and visual effects that hide first-paint information.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before assertion: important states should lead to the record, source, or calculation behind them.
- Operational clarity: each screen should make the next decision or action easy to identify.
- Customer-safe by default: internal workflow detail and unsupported metrics stay out of customer-facing views.
- One platform, distinct workflows: modules may have restrained identity accents, but shared geometry and interaction patterns should make the portal feel unified.
- Motion explains state: animation adds orientation and feedback without delaying access to information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA where practical. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access for interactive charts and controls, visible focus states without distracting rectangles, reduced-motion alternatives, responsive layouts, and non-color-only status cues.
