# SkyStation Operations HQ

## Purpose

SkyStation Operations HQ is Skylark Drones' operations intelligence portal for delivery, fleet readiness, reporting, crew capacity, analytics, billing evidence, and MIS.

## Public boundary

- `index.html` is the protected production portal shell. It must fail closed when authentication, authorization, backend configuration, or current source snapshots are unavailable.
- `design-preview.html` is an authentication-free, synthetic-only visual preview. Its people, customers, sites, assets, counts, percentages, and amounts are fictional.
- Do not infer live operational facts from the synthetic preview.
- Public dashboard projections must exclude confidential billing amounts, employee-level sensitive details, and internal Monday snapshots.

## Navigation

The portal contains Central Dashboard, Activity Tracking, Inventory, Crew Management, Analytics, Billing, and MIS modules. Display Mode rotates only the six Central Dashboard sheets; Deep Dive modules remain manually navigated.

## Source and data state

Production data is read-only from approved Monday boards through the protected backend. Missing, stale, invalid, or unconfigured sources are surfaced as explicit unavailable or review states; the frontend must never substitute demo fixtures for missing production data.
